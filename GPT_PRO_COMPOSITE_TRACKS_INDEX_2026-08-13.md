## Coordination rule

The seven outputs are competing, separately attributable proposals. They may be
reviewed in parallel. They must not be implemented as one combined arm merely
because all seven designs exist. First select designs that pass evidence,
feasibility, leakage, cost, and attribution review; only then preregister a
small number of prospective experiments.

The first comparison for every track is Full versus its own role-shadow, not a
naive ranking across tracks with different auxiliary budgets. Before live work,
freeze a common resource envelope if a direct seven-way ranking is desired;
otherwise report an accuracy-cost Pareto frontier. Unused budget is not filled
with dummy calls.
