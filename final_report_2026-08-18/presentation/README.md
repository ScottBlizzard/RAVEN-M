The V10 deck has no appendix and is organized for a first-time evaluator. It
first states what was done, then establishes A0, explains the A0→A1→R2 design,
summarizes the horizontal exploration, and presents the R2 result. Only after
the R2 residual failures and the R15 contradiction are understood does it
introduce the information opportunity chain, use it to re-read R5-R12, and
close with the literature position and validation boundary. V10 interprets the
full evidence through a three-level boundary: memory can preserve an existing
capability, reuse existing successful experience when donor coverage exists,
but did not by itself fill missing planning, perception, recovery, or
completion-grounding capabilities. It also states the decisive follow-up as an
expert-donor × memory ON/OFF controlled test. The deck retains the sealed
A4-v2 donor-acquisition result: 24 valid slots, 6 successes, 18 scientific
failures, and only 3 / 7 routes meeting donor coverage. This extends the audit
from L0-L6 online fact tracing to P0+L0-L6 precondition-aware attribution.
