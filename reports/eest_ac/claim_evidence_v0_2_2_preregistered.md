# EEST-AC v0.2.2 Preregistered Claim–Evidence Table

| ID | Claim | Minimum evidence | PASS criterion | Forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-C1 | One authoritative source closes the complete decision envelope. | Generated-artifact hashes plus top-level, phase, action, evidence, citation, intent, parser, repair, and adapter conformance rows. | Every frozen envelope rule is generated or exhaustively machine-checked with no uncovered field constraint. | Treating action-only conformance as full-envelope conformance. |
| E-C2 | Descriptive intent length cannot falsely reject an otherwise legal command. | v0.2.1 contaminated replay plus short, boundary, Unicode, high-entropy, and long-display tests. | Every non-empty string is accepted; whitespace/display normalization has raw hash/length and provenance; no metadata-only repair call occurs. | Claiming intent is ignored entirely or allowing missing/non-string/empty intent. |
| E-C3 | Control-plane fields remain fail closed. | Missing/extra/type/bound/phase tests for status, action, evidence, and citations, including repair traces. | Invalid authority-bearing fields never reach the adapter; at most one explicit repair; invalid repair terminates transparently. | Letting fail-soft metadata handling weaken action or evidence authorization. |
| E-C4 | Safe action normalization remains semantic and bounded. | Property/direction/boundary/no-clamp tests and frozen v0.2 replay corpus. | Only complete unique aliases normalize; no out-of-bounds endpoint, clamp, or `recent_app` substitution is accepted. | Calling semantic guessing deterministic normalization. |
| E-C5 | The real model can satisfy the complete envelope contract. | At most three newly frozen live probes after all offline gates. | 3/3 valid command within one control repair; 3/3 adapter/execute/state-change/reset; three categories; zero truncation; exact accounting; zero metadata-only repairs. | Any memory, M-SLOTS, M-RISK, or task-efficacy claim. |
| E-C6 | Failure-path accounting and early stop are complete. | Raw JSONL, attempts, per-probe summaries, unexecuted-cell records, and completion record. | Raw calls equal attempts and counters; first hard failure stops later probes and marks them not run. | Relabelling controller invalidity as infrastructure retry or silently replacing a probe. |
| E-C7 | Qualification isolation is preserved. | Source scan, v0.2.2 lock, contaminated-input declaration, legacy start/end hashes, process audit, and stop record. | No forbidden production branch; no old Q-SWIPE live reuse; no efficacy/M-RISK auto-start; legacy WIP unchanged and unstaged. | Treating qualification output as held-out efficacy evidence. |
| E-C8 | Required state change is measured by a frozen terminal settling window. | Raw zero-call positive/dynamic-negative/a11y-missing sample hashes plus live per-probe transition audit. | Final two screenshot+a11y+package signatures agree, a11y is available, and terminal state differs from pre; negatives are rejected. | Counting transition animation, camera frame churn, or missing a11y as stable task effect. |

Overall PASS means only “the shared decision-envelope controller contract is eligible for a separately preregistered next phase.” Overall FAIL means “remain at the controller floor.”
