# EEST-AC Claim–Evidence Verdict v0.1.1

This post-batch table evaluates the claims frozen in `claim_evidence_v0_1.md`. It does not replace or edit the preregistered table.

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence level | What the smoke actually showed |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 — typed slots preserve cross-page entity–field binding better than ordinary summary | **Mechanism signal only; task claim unsupported** | episode / paired-smoke | M-SLOTS and M-RISK admitted 4/4 correctly bound records for `Petar Muller → event_address → 968 Spruce…`, and the compiler routed the evidence. | C2 — GoalLedger does not invent requirements | **Supported for this implementation and smoke** | mechanical / paired-smoke | The live invented-requirement numerator was 0 over 8 root requirements. | C3 — Recovery entries are grounded in real no-effect | **Not supported in live execution** | episode | Seventeen recovery records were created. | C4 — current-screen authority dominates historical evidence | **Supported mechanically; no live contradiction observed** | mechanical / episode | Replay and corruption tests passed. | C5 — action-conditioned verification is selective | **Selectivity supported; efficacy untested** | mechanical / paired-smoke | M-RISK made 0 gate calls on 14 detector-ineligible candidates, so unnecessary verification was 0/14. | C6 — B3-MATCH is a fair call-schedule control | **Protocol/mechanism supported, realized counts diverged** | mechanical / paired-smoke | B3-MATCH used four useful summary calls at eligible Send opportunities and no neutral padding. M-RISK reached no eligible opportunity, so actual auxiliary calls were 4 versus 0 because trajectories diverged after treatment. Caps and trigger policy were matched; realized totals were not. |
| C7 — the minimal method improves task success | **Unsupported; expansion criterion failed** | paired-smoke | All arms scored 1/2 intent-to-treat: every arm passed the negative control and failed the positive task. | C8 — a full stale/superseded/revoked lifecycle is necessary | **Out of scope** | none | The new namespace intentionally did not implement or test the old lifecycle claim. No H17 evidence is reused. |
| C9 — memory reliability is a main AndroidWorld bottleneck | **Undecidable; early controller-floor warning** | episode / paired-smoke | B3 showed a real wrong-destination memory hazard, but one positive instance cannot estimate the preregistered 2% natural-hazard rate. Correctly stored evidence did not overcome navigation/grounding and completion failures. |

## Decision

Do not expand this seed to the 48-cell study. Repair only shared, task-agnostic controller and measurement defects, pass replay/corruption/delayed-transition/output/completion preflights, then select new held-out templates and seeds for another small paired smoke.

The completed SMS/Clock instances are now development replay cases and must not be presented as held-out evidence in the next study.
