## Horizontal memory-arm status

- A6 and A7 are complete 19-task comparison arms and useful negative controls.
- A10-v1, A10-v2, and A11 did not pass their formal offline qualification.
- A12 is formally `A12_PROTOCOL_INVALID`: independent replay found at most
  11/23 qualifying reference segments, below the preregistered 20/23 gate.
- Enriched six-task runs are post-hoc diagnostics. They do not repair or rename
  a failed formal arm and are not held-out evaluations.

The completed A10-v2 diagnostic scored 2/6 with six actual non-empty reads in
three episodes, but productive divergence count was zero. Both successful
episodes were memory-silent. See
`evidence/diag6/A10V2_DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT_2026-08-13.md`.

A11 and A12 diagnostic execution is complete. A11 scored 2/6 with four actual
reads in two failed episodes; its two successes were memory-silent. A12 scored
1/6 with three actual reads in two failed episodes; its only success was
memory-silent. Both had zero productive-divergence signals. A11's first
`OsmAndTrack` attempt remains preserved as infrastructure-invalid and is not
scientifically scored. See
`evidence/diag6/A11_A12_DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULTS_2026-08-13.md`.

For A11, authoritative activation evidence is native nested
`memory_mechanism.reads` plus diagnostic causal-read records. The generic
aggregate helper does not read that nested schema, so its top-level
`active_episode_count` requires reconciliation.

## Current research decision

The horizontal comparison is sufficiently broad. The next primary arm should
be a vertical refinement of A1: preserve its smallest plausible causal benefit
while removing duplication, stale reinforcement, and excess cost. It must be a
new prospective arm; frozen A1 evidence and code must not be edited.

The new arm must first preserve A0's four full-success tasks, then preserve A1's
unique gain, and only then expand to all 19 tasks. Accuracy, cost, and mechanism
causality are separate claims.
