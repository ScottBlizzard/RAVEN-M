# A1-R12 Compacted-History Pending — Prospective Preregistration

A1-R12 is frozen after A1-R11 showed that coordinate self-checks can repair individual taps while repeated ordinary-history prose continues to reinforce a failed route. R12 preserves R11 and changes only the model-visible ordinary history: adjacent entries with the same whitespace-collapsed, quote-stripped, case-folded text are represented once, retaining the newest original text. Raw executed actions and full audit history are never removed or altered.

The transformation is deterministic, episode-local, task-agnostic, adds no call, does not use pixels/evaluator/UI tree/task identity, and cannot block, override, retry, or terminate actions. The fixed six-task capability gate stops on its first valid scientific failure. Only 6/6 releases the remaining 13 tasks. This identity cannot be tuned after a generated call.
